    You are teaching the app to see drumsticks
    
  
    For every hit in the video you mark both sticks twice — once when the moving stick is at its highest, and once when it lands. A stick is two clicks: its tip, then its back end.
    
  
    
    
    1. Mark the drum. Click its left edge, then its right edge, and say how wide it is. That gives the app a ruler.
    
    
    2. The top of each stroke. Step through the video until the stick is at its highest. Click its tip, then its back end; then the other stick's tip and back end.
    
    
    3. The hit. The same four clicks on the picture where the tip touches the drum. Then on to the next stroke.
    
  
    
  
    The panel on the right always says what to click next. Nothing can be broken: Undo takes back any click.
